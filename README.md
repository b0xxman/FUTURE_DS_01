# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes business sales performance using the Superstore Sales dataset.

The objective is to identify sales trends, profitable categories, and regional performance to support business decision-making.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- GitHub

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset

## Dashboard KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit

## Visualizations
1. Sales by Month
2. Sales by Region
3. Sales by Category
4. Region Filter (Slicer)

## Key Insights

### Sales Trend
Sales generally increased throughout the year, with stronger performance during the final months.

### Regional Performance
The West region generated the highest sales, while the South region generated the lowest sales.

### Category Performance
Technology contributed the largest share of sales compared to other categories.

## Business Recommendations
- Increase focus on high-performing regions such as West and East.
- Expand marketing efforts for Technology products.
- Investigate reasons for lower sales in the South region.
- Plan promotional campaigns during high-performing months to maximize revenue.
